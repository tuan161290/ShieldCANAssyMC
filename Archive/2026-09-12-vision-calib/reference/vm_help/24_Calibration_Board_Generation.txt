 Calibration Board Generation

 The Calibration Board Generation Tool is used to generate calibration
 board images based on customized parameters. After obtaining the generated calibration board
 images, you can modify or print them to further calibrate the camera as
 necessary.

 Ensure that you have collected all necessary information related to the use of a
 calibration board, such as the working distance of the camera, accuracy requirements
 of specific projects, and materials of the calibration board, among others. This can
 help you generate calibration borad images that meet the specific requirements. 

- 
 On the
 menu bar, go to Tools > Calibration Board Generation to open the tool window.

- 
 Enter a calibration board type and press Enter. 
 
 Note 

- 
 For the specific differences between Hikrobot I
 Calibration Board and Hikrobot II Calibration Board, refer to
 CalibBoard Calibration.

- 
 The difference between the standard and extended
 calibration board is that the standard type supports a maximum
 of 255 rows and columns, while the extended type supports a
 maximum of 1,000 rows and columns.

- 
 According to the prompts, enter the values of the following parameters: the
 number of rows, the number of columns, the length of the grid, whether to
 generate a CAD drawing, and whether to generate mark numbers. In the ideal
 situations, the calibration board should cover the field of view of the camera. 
 
 Note 
 The values of calibration board parameters may vary depending on several
 factors, such as the accuracy requirements of specific projects, the
 quality and material of the calibration board, and the field of view of
 the camera.

- 
 After entering the corresponding parameter values, press
 Enter to generate the corresponding calibration board
 drawing (in BMP format) and CAD drawing (in DXF format).
 
 CAUTION

 The calibration board drawing can only be generated when the number of
 rows and columns of that calibration board is less than 50. Since the
 drawing file is only for preview, do not print and use that drawing in
 your projects.

- 
 Navigate to the installation path of the VM
 Platform to obtain the generated drawings.
 
 The generated calibration board drawing and CAD drawing (.dxf
 format files) are saved under the VM Platform VM installation path:
 .\Applications\Tools\CalibrationBoardGenerationTool.

 Note 
 These drawings cannot be directly used in your projects. To use them, you
 need to fill the grids with colors first.

 After obtaining the drawing of the calibration board, you need to manually fill the
 grids with colors and adjust them as necessary.

 Note 

- 
 When coloring the grids of the calibration board, you need to select
 a proper color according to the light source, the material of the
 calibration board and other related factors in your use case.

- 
 When processing the drawing, you need to ensure its aspect ratio is
 not changed, the unit of grid is correct, and the drawing is not
 rasterized. Then, you need to re-examine the calibration parameters
 to ensure the calibration results are accurate and meet your
 requirements.
