Several conclusions on positioning and guiding 
typical scenarios
 1. Total deviation of positioning guidance=rotation matrix × The deviation from the teaching Mark point to
the center of rotation + the deviation from the running Mark point to the reference Mark point.
 2. The non merging of MARK points and mechanical grasping point centers does not affect the positioning
calculation results, as the relative position relationship between image positioning MARK points and grasping
centers is rigid and independent of rotation.
 3. When the Mark point of the image coincides with the center of rotation of the machine, there will be no
deviation in rotation. Therefore, there is no need to pay attention to the position of the rotation center, and
there is no need for a 12 point calibration with rotation. Instead, a 9 point calibration with only translation can
be used.
 4. The so-called non coaxial refers to the fact that the Mark point and rotation center of the image do not
coincide. When non coaxial occurs, it is only necessary to consider the additional deviation caused by rotation.
The calculation process of positioning guidance is how to calculate the deviation


===== PAGE 40 =====
Several conclusions on the alignment of 
upper and bottom camera mapping
 1. Be sure to unify to the same coordinate system before performing operations. Map the
image coordinates of the upper camera to the coordinate system of the bottom camera, and
then calculate the deviation from the reference point to the running point in the image
coordinate system of the lower camera.
 2. Attention should be paid to the mapping of the angle of the upper camera image to the
angle of the lower camera, and the corresponding relationship between the points before and
after the mapping should be noted.
 3. The camera also indirectly taught while mapping, killing two birds with one stone.
 4. The calculation process of positioning guidance is similar, which calculates the deviation
from the teaching position to the operating position.
The mapping process not only involves image conversion between cameras, but also teaching!
